BINAURAL BEATS

A gentle tonal focus

These use slightly different tones in the left and right channels. Stereo headphones are needed to hear the binaural effect. Research on benefits is mixed; use these as a relaxation aid, not as a treatment.

🌙 Delta · 2 HzOften associated with deep-sleep sound sessions
💤 Theta · 6 HzFor a quiet, dreamy wind-down
🌿 Alpha · 10 HzFor relaxed, calm listening
🧘 Low beta · 14 HzA slightly more alert, steady tone

Keep the volume low. Don't listen while driving or doing anything that needs full attention. Stop if you feel uncomfortable.

A KINDER KIND OF MINDFULNESS

There's no wrong way to begin.

Quiet Space is a small, free place to pause. No streaks, no pressure, no subscription.

Try this when everything feels like too much

  1. Put both feet on the floor, if that feels comfortable.
  2. Let your shoulders drop a little.
  3. Notice one thing you can see, one thing you can feel, and one thing you can hear.
  4. Remind yourself: “I only need to take the next small step.”

Mindfulness isn't about forcing yourself to feel calm. It's about meeting yourself where you are.

Quiet Space is for general wellness and isn't a substitute for professional mental-health care.
